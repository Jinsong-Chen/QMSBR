One question, two linked arguments, three perspectives

The Argument-Based Framework for Quantitative Research places a quantitative question at the centre and keeps it connected to its answer. STAT-E–STAT-C build an intermediate bounded statistical claim from empirical evidence, statistical justification, inference, checking, and uncertainty. The substantive argument develops the same four roles in context: SUB-E situates STAT-E, SUB-J augments and evaluates STAT-J, SUB-I interprets STAT-I, and STAT-C bounds and calibrates SUB-C. Argument, Workflow, and Evaluation are three coordinated views of this same reasoning. The framework is Toulmin-informed; Chapter 1’s Section 6, How the Regression-Based Approach Develops and Instantiates the Framework, shows both how regression expands the available targets, comparisons, models, and checks and how a particular analysis fills the eight components. The subsequent chapter map supports that explanation.

Designed for more than one course

The chapter identities and public links stay stable while instructors choose a route that fits their students. QMSBR provides the canonical released readings and, when separately approved in later releases, computing resources. Moodle or another course system can hold the term sequence, classroom notes, announcements, and assessment.

Current, not duplicated
One released version at each stable URL, with recoverable history through Git and tagged releases.

Traceable reasoning
Examples keep design, evidence, uncertainty, interpretation, and limitations connected to the question each chapter addresses.

Selective publication
Public chapters and examples are separated from instructor materials and course-specific teaching assets.

What comes next

Part One will extend through interaction, generalized linear models, prediction, and a credibility capstone. Part Two is reserved for factor analysis/SEM, and Part Three for multilevel/longitudinal models. Part Four and later collections can join this same site when their scope is clear and real material is ready.
