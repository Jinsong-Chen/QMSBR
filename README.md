# QMSBR

**Quantitative Methods for Social and Behavioral Research** — the public
Quarto site at <https://jinsong-chen.github.io/QMSBR/>: the part
introductions and concept references, the published chapters with their
PDFs, and the R support pages under `practice/`.

The plan for this site — navigation, pages, theme, build and freeze, and how
to add a chapter — is [WEBSITE_PLAN.md](WEBSITE_PLAN.md).

not an official University service.
