This reference gives one agreed meaning for each concept the twelve chapters of Part Two use. It exists so that a reader who meets a term in Chapter 3 can find the chapter and section that explains it, and so that the chapters need not define a term again each time they use it. The concept tables gather related terms into broad groups and give each term’s meaning or essential distinction; the closing table records the notation the chapters share. It is written for lookup rather than for reading through. Chapters 1–4 are available on the website; references to Chapters 5–12 point forward to chapters that are not yet available.

A row is read from left to right. The preferred name comes first, with the common alternatives a reader will meet elsewhere beside it. The meaning cell states the essential distinction rather than a full explanation. The final cell of a concept table, Main explanation, is a destination: the chapter and section where the term is principally explained, with a first use elsewhere noted where one exists. In the notation table the Main use column carries that destination and a further column records alternative symbols and the cautions attached to them. Terms that name the same concept in context share an entry, related concepts with different meanings have separate entries, and a symbol whose preferred form carries a qualifier takes a row of its own that names its other uses. This reference records the vocabulary rather than teaching it: first-use explanations, worked examples and the reasons behind each distinction stay in the chapters themselves.
